Deauth

Sends deauthentication frames to disconnect clients from their access point. Requires a selected target. Works on devices that don't implement 802.11w.

Beacon

Broadcasts fake access point advertisements. Configure SSIDs in the SSIDs tab.

Probe

Sends probe requests as if looking for known networks, confusing WiFi trackers. Requires SSIDs to be configured.
